# Decision: Mascot presence on clinician surfaces

**Captain's answer (2026-08-08):** Duolingo/Wysa-like in kind, but deliberately less present
than either. "Sort of like Duolingo or Wysa, just not AS present."

**Interpretation adopted:** the seafoam elephant is a recurring, recognisable companion with
personality and multiple poses — not a one-off logo — but it is dosed well below a
gamified-consumer level and stays off data-dense clinical surfaces.

**Concrete rule (from `data/aira-ui-s3/report.md` §A.3 / §A.9, now confirmed):**

- **Present on human surfaces:** unlock/setup, home, empty states, onboarding, the
  audio-deleted trust moment, patient-facing journal.
- **Banned from:** the chart plot area, the caseload table, and the risk queue.
- No idle nagging, no streaks, no celebratory interruptions, no reaction to clinical values.
- One ambient motion only (the 5.5s float). Nothing bounces or springs.

The mascot is the app's entire warmth budget; because it carries the charm, clinical surfaces
stay sober without reading as cold.
